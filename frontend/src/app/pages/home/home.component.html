RevHire Job Portal

Connect job seekers and employers with profile management, resume workflow, smart search, and application tracking.

Job Seeker Features

  • Register/Login and profile management
  • Text resume builder and resume upload
  • Advanced job search and one-click apply
  • Application tracking and withdrawal

Employer Features

  • Company registration and account login
  • Create/manage job postings
  • Review, shortlist, reject applicants
  • Dashboard with hiring stats
